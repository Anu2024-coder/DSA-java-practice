A Graph is a data structure made of:

Vertices (Nodes) → points
Edges → connections between nodes

Types of Graphs--
1)Based on Direction
Undirected Graph(bidirected) → A — B
Directed Graph (Digraph) → A → B

2)Based on Weight
Unweighted → edges have no cost
Weighted → edges have weights (distance, cost) can be negative also

Special Types
Cyclic Graph → contains cycle
Acyclic Graph (DAG) → no cycle
Connected Graph
Disconnected Graph


Storing a graph-
1)Adjacency list - lists of lists (optimized in terms of time and space) 
examples- ArrayList<ArrayList>
Array<ArrayList> *preferred
HashMap<int,lists> where int-vertex(key) and lists-value
2)Adjacency matrix

3)Edge list
4)2D matrix(Implicit graph)
